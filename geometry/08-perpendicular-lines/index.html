When do two lines meet at 90°?

Change the slope to tilt a line. Change the intercept to move it. Watch what happens to the angle.

Controls range from −10 to 10. Type decimals for precise values. For vertical lines, the position control sets x = c; the slope is undefined.

A slope gives a direction, not a complete line.

In y = mx + b, m is the slope and b is where the line crosses the y-axis. The starting example sets both intercepts to zero, but perpendicular lines can have any intercepts.

Try Different intercepts: the lines still meet at 90°. For two finite slopes, perpendicular means m₁ × m₂ = −1. Horizontal and vertical lines are the special case.
