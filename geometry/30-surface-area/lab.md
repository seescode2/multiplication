# Lab: Surface Area

## Main idea

Surface area is the total area covering every outside face of a solid. A rectangular prism has three matching pairs of faces.

## Learning goal

Add all six faces of a rectangular prism.

## Run the simulation

You need [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) installed. From this folder, run:

```bash
node app.js 4 3 2
```

The numbers after `app.js` are inputs. Change them, run the command again, and compare the output. No packages or setup are needed.

## Directions

1. Run the example and add the three subtotals.
2. Try a 2-by-2-by-2 cube.
3. Compare surface area and volume units. Why are they different?


## Think about it

- What stayed the same when you changed an input?
- What changed, and why?
- Write one sentence explaining the main idea in your own words.

## Success check

You are ready to move on when you can predict one output before running the simulation and explain whether your prediction was correct.
